About MileForge

MileForge is a Canadian web-based trucking load management and profit calculation platform. It is designed for owner-operators, small trucking fleets, semi-truck drivers and hotshot operators using pickup trucks with trailers.

Manage your loads, calculate estimated trip profitability, track expenses and invoices, and organize day-to-day hauling activity from one workspace.

What MileForge helps you do

  • Estimate trip revenue and costs, including distance and operating expenses.
  • Track hauling loads from planning through delivery and payment status.
  • Record expenses and prepare invoices.
  • Browse loads posted by MileForge community members, subject to review and availability.
  • Use vehicle and trailer information to assess preliminary load compatibility.

For carriers and shippers

Carriers can keep a clearer view of their numbers and workload. Shippers can post freight opportunities for review and connect with interested carriers through the MileForge community.

Canada and cross-border operations

MileForge is built with Canadian owner-operators and cross-border trucking businesses in mind. Freight listings, carrier credentials and external partner integrations depend on availability and authorization. MileForge does not guarantee bookings, freight availability or revenue.
